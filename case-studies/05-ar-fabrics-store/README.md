## What it includes

**For shoppers**
- Brand-led home page and collections (lawn, organza, cambric and more)
- Shop-all page with filters and sorting by price, fabric and piece count
- Product pages with colours, sizes and **live stock**
- Slide-out shopping bag with a free-delivery progress bar
- Checkout and **order tracking**
- A journal (blog) for the brand's story and SEO

**For the brand owner**
- Admin panel for products, stock, orders and content

**Built for growth**
- Fast server-rendered pages that search engines can read
- Mobile-first, because most customers shop on their phones

## Technology

`Next.js 15` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma`
